Social Media Recommendation Puzzle

Classification Task

Scenario:

Xiao Ming watched 3 basketball videos and liked them on Douyin. The platform then recommended the following content. Identify which are directly related recommendations vs potential risks:

A

More NBA highlight clips

B

Ads for sports protective gear

C

A controversial short video: "Basketball stars earn far more than scientists"

D

Notification for a local basketball game registration

Classification Task:

Directly related:
Drop letters here
Potential risks:
Drop letters here

Answer:

Directly related: A, B, D

Potential risks: C (May trigger value bias)

Autonomous Driving Ethical Dilemma

Fill-in-the-Blank

Scenario:

An autonomous vehicle malfunctions and must choose between:

  1. Swerving to hit a motorcyclist who ran a red light (1 person)
  2. Proceeding straight and hitting a compliant elderly couple crossing the road (2 people)

Question 1:

Human drivers typically rely on ______ to make such decisions

Question 2:

If the AI chooses Option 1 based on the "minimal casualty principle", it may violate the ______ principle

Answer:

1. Immediate intuition

2. Traffic regulations (Because the motorcyclist's violation should bear responsibility)
