But despite all this incredible power, a standard AI like ChatGPT still has one major limitation: It is passive. It sits in a chat window, waiting for you to type a prompt. It can write a recipe for you, but it cannot go to the grocery store and buy the ingredients.

Let's meet your new digital workforce!
