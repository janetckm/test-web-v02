In the last chapter, we uncovered the basics of AI—what it is, how it evolved, and how it's already part of your daily life.

Now, it's time to see how those foundations come to life in the real world. This chapter moves from the laboratory to the marketplace, showing how digital intelligence is transforming the industries that drive our daily lives.

We will look at how it manages risk and trading in finance, how it personalizes shopping and predicts trends in retail, and how it powers automation and safety in manufacturing.

Together, these examples show that digital intelligence is not just a technological topic — it is a practical force reshaping how business value is created, how decisions are made, and how entire industries evolve.
